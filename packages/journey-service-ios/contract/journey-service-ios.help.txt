Usage:
  journey-service-ios ios check <config.yaml> [--service <key>] [--require-locale <locale>] [--github-output]

Commands:
  ios check  Check that the iOS Simulator target in services.<key>.options has a runtime and
             device type that agree. Prints a confirmation line.
             --require-locale also requires the target's locale pin to equal <locale>, for a
             caller whose models depend on it; no locale is assumed without it.
             --github-output writes simulator, device-type and runtime to GITHUB_OUTPUT, for
             a step that selects or caches the simulator runtime.
             Defaults: --service ios.

Exit codes:
  0  Success.
  1  The configuration failed the check.
  2  Invalid arguments.
